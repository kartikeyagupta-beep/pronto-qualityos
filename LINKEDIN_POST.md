# LinkedIn launch post

Quality problems rarely begin with “bad people”. They begin with weak signals, fragmented feedback, and interventions that are never measured.

Today we’re sharing a public prototype of **Pronto QualityOS** — a command centre for turning service data into better customer outcomes.

With QualityOS, an operations team can:

• see quality health across professionals, services, cities and micro-markets  
• identify who needs attention, without treating every low score the same  
• connect complaints to likely root causes using booking evidence  
• assign the right refresher or coaching intervention  
• monitor the next set of bookings  
• measure whether behaviour and customer outcomes actually improved

### How to use the prototype

1. Open **Command Centre** for the overall quality picture and priority queue.
2. Select a professional to inspect their score, evidence and likely root cause.
3. Assign a targeted intervention from the profile.
4. Use **Root Cause** to see how feedback becomes an operational diagnosis.
5. Review **Interventions**, **Training** and **Outcomes** to close the loop.

### How Pronto can use this

QualityOS can sit above booking, complaint, refund, rating and training data. Each day it can surface the smallest number of actions with the highest expected customer impact. Over time, it becomes a learning system: which behaviours create which outcomes, which coaching works, and where the operating system—not the individual—needs to change.

The goal is simple: **fix systems, coach precisely, and prove what worked.**

This public version uses synthetic demo data. I’d love feedback from operators building high-frequency, frontline service businesses.
